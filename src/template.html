Notes. Crater ages are uncertain. When only a range is known, the crater is placed at the midpoint. When only an upper limit is known (e.g. "less than 60 Ma"), it is placed at that limit, so it may be younger than shown. Fireball impact energy is NASA's estimate from the radiated light. Sensor coverage has changed over time, so the yearly counts are not a clean trend.
